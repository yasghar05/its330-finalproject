Paris

Historical Overview

Just like Tokyo, Paris is also a capital city, but this time for France. Also, with more comparisons, Paris also has a rich history that dates back up to 2,000 years ago, and it also ties into the city being named something else originally. This time, for Paris, it was a small settlement known as Lutetia, but during Ancient Roman times, more on that later, it became Paris. Bringing in more Japanese comparisons, under this new name the city was able to grow artistically, politically, and even culturally, and this is highlighted in many of the attractions one may see here in Paris. After all, Paris is not called the "City of Light" for nothing, philosophically speaking.

Top 5 Attractions:

  1. Eiffel Tower
  2. Louvre Museum
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral
  4. Montmartre Hill
  5. Champs-Élysées

Cultural Significance (Why Is It Heavily Visited?)

So, Paris' cultural significance on why it is so heavily visited can be chalked up to it being a culturally global artistic and fashion-based country influencing all forms of multimedia with a bright, cultural lifestyle found in almost every corner whether one goes to one of the most visited museums, cafes, streets, or even spends their time raving at festivals or shopping down the famed stores the country is well-known for being home too. Really, Paris is a treat in it of itself, one may say!
