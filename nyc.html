New York City

Historical Overview

Like a lot of these most visited cities in the world, they all started out somewhere differently from where they ended up, already taking heed of this with the name. New York City started as a Dutch trading post known as New Amsterdam in 1624, but it was then taken over by the UK alongside the rest of the US East Coast as the 13 Colonies. Thus, the New York we all know and love was born, and from that led into the usual worldwide spread of multiculturalism, finance, arts, multimedia, and much more. Read down below to learn more about the Big Apple itself.

Top 5 Attractions:

  1. Statue of Liberty
  2. Times Square
  3. Central Park
  4. Empire State Building
  5. Brooklyn Bridge

Cultural Significance (Why Is It Heavily Visited?)

Well, NYC is one of the most culturally diverse cities worldwide being a global leader in multimedia of art, fashion, music, plays, and one of the most diverse areas of people from Eastern Europeans to Hispanic neighborhoods. With this, one could call the cities within the state city-states, or cities within cities if you will. Also, that statement truly rings a notch when one ultra analyzes all the landmarks in entertainment, food, history, and other wonderful experiences this wonderful city offers all-year-round rain, sun, or snow.
