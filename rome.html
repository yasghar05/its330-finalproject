Rome

Historical Overview

Rome's backstory is a little different than the other cities mentioned on this site. For Rome, it was founded in 753 BC, and according to legend it grew from a small settlement of the now defunct Roman Empire into one of the strongest ancient civilizations out there. But, even when the Empire fell, the city stayed essential due to it being the center of the Catholic Church, ultimately growing into Italy's capital city and another place where the old meets the new, a la Japan.

Top 5 Attractions:

  1. Colosseum
  2. The Forum
  3. The Pantheon
  4. Trevi Fountain
  5. Vatican City

Cultural Significance (Why Is It Heavily Visited?)

So, Rome is known as "The Eternal City" due to its influence of history from Catholicism to its infamous governmental systems and even some more multimedia with the arts of both painting and plays, but who could even forget the food, Italian blends, and once more the old meeting up with the new?
